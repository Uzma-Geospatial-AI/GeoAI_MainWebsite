About Us

Turning space data into certainty for Earth.

Geospatial AI Sdn Bhd, founded in 2021, is a geospatial analytics company and a wholly owned subsidiary of Uzma Berhad. Our expertise lies in utilizing satellite imagery and cutting-edge technology to provide actionable insights about various aspects of Earth — from urban planning to environmental monitoring.

Our Vision

To become a top-3 geospatial analytics company in Southeast Asia, providing near real-time insights for any surface on Earth by 2030.

Our Mission

Empower decision-makers and industries with accurate, actionable insights through cutting-edge geospatial analytics — contributing to Environmental, Social and Governance (ESG) goals for sustainable development.

The Game Changer

UzmaSAT-1 — a historic milestone.

Malaysia's first commercial very high-resolution Earth Observation ("EO") satellite was deployed into low Earth orbit via SpaceX's Falcon 9 Transporter-12 at 4:06am on 15 January 2025 — 57 minutes 35 seconds after launching from Vandenberg Space Force Base, California.
